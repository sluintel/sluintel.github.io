Why LIC OFS Is Dominating 2026 Market Talk

This isn't just another IPO; it's a secondary market offering where existing shares are sold.

Expert Playbook: Pro Tips for Engaging with LIC OFS

Navigating an OFS requires a blend of diligence and strategic thinking. Here are some insider tips to help you make informed decisions about LIC OFS.

  • Don't Rush Your Bid: Unlike IPOs, OFS often sees heavy bidding on the last day. Placing your bid too early might lock you into a price without seeing the full subscription demand.
  • Utilize the Cut-Off Price Option: If you're confident in the stock but uncertain about the final price, select the "cut-off price" option. This ensures you get shares at the final determined price for non-retail categories, which can often be slightly more favorable.
  • Understand the Retail Quota: Always be aware of the specific quota reserved for retail investors. This can influence your chances of allocation, especially in oversubscribed offers.
  • Focus on the Fundamentals: While the OFS discount is tempting, your primary reason for investment should be LIC's long-term business prospects, its financial health, and its position in the competitive insurance landscape.
