Bayern Munich Dominance: The Unstoppable Goal Machine

In 2014, a controversial free transfer saw him move to rivals Bayern Munich, where he elevated his game to an unprecedented level. This period solidified his status as one of the best strikers ever.

Seeking a new challenge, Lewandowski moved to FC Barcelona in 2022. Many questioned if he could replicate his Bayern form in a new league at his age.
