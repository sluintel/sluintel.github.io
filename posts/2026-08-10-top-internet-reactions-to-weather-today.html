Pro Tips for Navigating Weather Reactions (and Avoiding Pitfalls)

Navigating the digital storm of weather chatter isn't always straightforward. Here's how to do it right and sidestep common blunders:

  • Be Authentic, Not Opportunistic: Don't jump on severe weather solely for brand visibility. Offer genuine help or resources if you're a local business.
  • Context is King: A snow day meme works in Minnesota but might be tone-deaf during a blizzard in an unprepared region. Always consider local impact.

  • Don't Be a Broken Record: If everyone's complaining about the heat, your generic "it's hot!" tweet won't stand out. Find a unique angle or offer a solution.
