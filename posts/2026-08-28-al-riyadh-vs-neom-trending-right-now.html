Riyadh is the beating heart of Saudi Arabia, its political and economic center. The strategy here is about supercharging an existing, vast city. Think monumental infrastructure projects, new financial districts, a burgeoning tech scene, and cultural revitalization. It's about taking a significant global city and catapulting it into the top ten most competitive and livable cities worldwide. This involves massive investment in public transport, green spaces, and a concerted effort to attract multinational corporations.

  • Focus on transforming existing infrastructure.

  • Aims to increase population to 15-20 million by 2030.
  • Home to government ministries and the established business community.
