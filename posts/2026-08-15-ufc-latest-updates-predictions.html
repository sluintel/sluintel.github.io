Even with advanced tools, human error can derail your predictions. Here are some actionable tips to sharpen your edge and sidestep common mistakes.

  • Avoid Chasing Hype: Media narratives can be powerful. Don't let a fighter's "story" or charisma override their technical capabilities and statistical performance. Logic, not emotion, should guide your analysis.
  • Understand AI Limitations: AI is powerful but lacks intuition. It can't account for a fighter's emotional resilience after a personal tragedy or a sudden surge of adrenaline. Always blend AI insights with your qualitative understanding.
