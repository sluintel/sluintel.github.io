NEPSE Mastery: Insider Tips & Traps to Avoid

Even with all the data, successful investing comes down to discipline and avoiding common pitfalls. Here's my take on what separates consistently profitable investors from those who struggle.

  • Mistake 1: Chasing "Hot" Stocks Without Research. Just because everyone's talking about a stock doesn't make it right for you. Always do your due diligence on company financials and prospects.
  • Mistake 2: Ignoring Regulatory Announcements. SEBON and NEPSE circulars contain vital information about market rules or new offerings. Disregarding them leaves you vulnerable to sudden shifts.
  • Mistake 3: Over-Leveraging on Margin. Margin trading amplifies both gains and losses. Use it extremely cautiously, and only if you fully understand the risks and have a solid exit strategy. Most retail investors should avoid it.
  • Mistake 4: Emotional Trading. Fear of missing out (FOMO) leads to buying at peaks, and panic selling locks in losses. Develop a trading plan and stick to it, regardless of daily noise.
  • Pro Tip: Invest in Learning Continuously. The market evolves, and your knowledge should too. Read, follow reputable analysts, and analyze your own trades. Your best asset isn't your capital; it's your informed decision-making.
