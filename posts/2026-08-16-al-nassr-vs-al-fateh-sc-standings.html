Goal Difference: More Than Just Goals

Goal difference (GD) is often overlooked but crucial. It's the total number of goals a team has scored minus the total number they've conceded. A high positive GD indicates not just a winning team, but a dominant one, capable of both prolific scoring and stingy defense. When teams are tied on points, GD is usually the first tie-breaker, making every goal scored or conceded potentially season-defining. Always check this metric when comparing closely ranked teams.

Head-to-Head Records: The Ultimate Tie-Breaker

After goal difference, many leagues, including the Saudi Pro League, use head-to-head results as a tie-breaker. This means if Al-Nassr and Al Fateh SC are level on points and have similar goal differences, their direct matches against each other become paramount. Did one team win both games?
