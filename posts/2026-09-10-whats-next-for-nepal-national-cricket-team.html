Strengthening Domestic Cricket

A strong international team is built on a robust local foundation. Nepal's current domestic structure, while passionate, needs professionalisation.

  • Professional Leagues: Establish multiple tiers of professional and semi-professional leagues across the country, providing consistent competitive opportunities for a wider pool of players.
  • Grassroots Development: Invest heavily in school and university cricket programs, ensuring a consistent pipeline of young talent. Create academies with certified coaches in every province.
  • Player Contracts & Welfare: Introduce competitive contracts for domestic players, offering financial stability and career pathways beyond the national team. Prioritise sports science, nutrition, and mental health support.

Investing in Infrastructure and Coaching

You can't expect world-class performance without world-class facilities and guidance. This is a non-negotiable step for Nepal.

  • Modern Training Facilities: Develop at least one state-of-the-art national cricket academy equipped with indoor nets, gymnasiums, recovery facilities, and specialist pitches catering to different conditions.
  • Certified Coaching Pathway: Implement an ICC-accredited coaching pathway program, not just for the national team, but for all levels of domestic cricket. Focus on specialist coaching for batting, bowling, and fielding.
  • Data Analytics & Sports Science: Embrace modern sports science. Provide access to video analysis tools, GPS trackers, and sports psychologists to enhance player performance and injury prevention.
