What is the Leagues Cup and Why it Matters Right Now

The Leagues Cup is more than just another football tournament; it's a monumental clash between Major League Soccer (MLS) and Liga MX, the top professional leagues in the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Instituted as an official CONCACAF tournament in 2023, it sees every team from both leagues compete in a World Cup-style format, culminating in a dramatic final. This structure amplifies rivalries, creates unprecedented matchups, and ultimately crowns a regional champion, offering coveted berths in the CONCACAF Champions Cup.

  • Unrivaled Regional Showdown: It’s the only tournament where every MLS and Liga MX club faces off, guaranteeing intense, high-stakes football.
