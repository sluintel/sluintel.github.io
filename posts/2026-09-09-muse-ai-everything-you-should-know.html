Getting Started with Muse: A Step-by-Step Guide to Unleashing Its Power

Ready to transform your digital workspace? Here’s a simple pathway to get you up and running with Muse AI.

  1. Download & First Canvas Setup: Start by downloading the Muse app (available on iPadOS/macOS, with web access developing). Your first interaction will be a blank canvas. Begin by creating a central idea or project title as your first card. Drag and drop a relevant document or link to get started.

  2. Importing & Connecting Ideas: Muse excels at bringing in diverse content. Drag PDFs, images, web links, and files from local storage directly onto your board. Use drawing tools to connect related items with arrows or create visual groupings. This is where your spatial thinking begins to take shape.

  3. Leveraging AI Prompts & Assistants: Select a group of cards or a specific document. Look for the AI assistant prompt – it might offer to summarize, extract keywords, or suggest follow-up questions. Experiment! Ask it to compare two ideas or brainstorm related concepts. The AI is your co-pilot for thinking.

  4. Organizing & Sharing Your Boards: As your boards grow, use nested boards or collections to maintain order. Muse makes sharing easy too; export boards as PDFs, images, or invite collaborators for real-time teamwork. This is perfect for team brainstorming or presenting complex ideas visually.

Expanding Your Muse AI Horizon: Key Integrations and Resources

While Muse is powerful on its own, it truly shines when integrated into a broader digital ecosystem.

  • Obsidian: Complements Muse by providing a robust backend for your textual knowledge, while Muse handles the visual and conceptual side. Best for those who love structured plain-text notes.
