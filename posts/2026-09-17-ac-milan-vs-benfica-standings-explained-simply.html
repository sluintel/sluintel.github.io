Points System: The Foundation

This is where it all begins. Most football competitions, particularly UEFA's Champions League and Europa League, adhere to a universal points system:

  • Win: 3 points
  • Draw: 1 point
  • Loss: 0 points

It sounds simple, right? But the true test of standings knowledge comes when two or more teams accumulate the same number of points.

Tie-Breakers: Unpacking the Nuances

This is where many casual fans get lost, and it's a critical component for understanding AC Milan vs Benfica standings when they're neck and neck. European competitions have a specific hierarchy of tie-breakers:

  1. Head-to-Head Points: The first and most crucial tie-breaker. If two teams are tied, their direct results against each other take precedence. The team with more points from their matches against each other ranks higher.
  2. Head-to-Head Goal Difference: If head-to-head points are equal, the goal difference in matches between those specific tied teams comes next.
  3. Head-to-Head Goals Scored: Still tied? The team that scored more goals in the head-to-head matches gets the nod.
  4. Away Goals in Head-to-Head: (Less common now but still relevant in some formats) If still tied, the number of away goals scored in the head-to-head matches can be a decider.
  5. Overall Goal Difference: Only if teams are still inseparable after all head-to-head criteria, then the overall goal difference across *all* group matches is considered.
  6. Overall Goals Scored: If the overall goal difference is identical, the team with more goals scored across all group matches ranks higher.
  7. Disciplinary Points (Fair Play): This is a rare, but possible, tie-breaker where yellow and red cards are tallied. Fewer disciplinary points (better fair play record) ranks higher.
  8. UEFA Club Coefficient: In the rarest of rare cases, if all else fails, the club with the higher UEFA club coefficient ranking typically takes precedence.
