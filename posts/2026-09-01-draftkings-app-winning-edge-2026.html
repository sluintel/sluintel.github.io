Beyond the Basics: Pro Tips & Common DraftKings App Mistakes

Even seasoned players stumble. Here’s how you can avoid common pitfalls and elevate your play to a truly professional level within the DraftKings app.

  • Chasing Losses: The biggest bankroll killer. If you have a losing day, walk away. Don't try to recoup your money by placing more impulsive bets.
  • Ignoring Line Shopping: While the DraftKings app offers competitive odds, it's rare they'll always have the absolute best line. For significant wagers, a quick check of other regulated sportsbooks can save you percentage points that add up over time.
  • Overlooking DFS Contest Selection: Not all contests are created equal. High-entry, large-field tournaments are harder to win. Mix in smaller-field contests, head-to-heads, and 50/50s for more consistent, lower-variance returns.
  • Betting on Your Favorite Team Blindly: Emotional betting is rarely profitable. Objectivity is paramount. If your favorite team's opponent offers strong value, take it, even if it feels wrong.
  • Not Using Responsible Gaming Tools: DraftKings offers self-exclusion, deposit limits, and spend limits. Use them. It's a sign of a smart, disciplined player, not a weak one.
