The Point System Explained

La Liga follows the standard football point system:

  • Win: 3 points
  • Draw: 1 point
  • Loss: 0 points

Consistency, therefore, is king. Atlético Madrid, historically known for their defensive solidity under Simeone, often grind out wins, while Real Sociedad typically play a more expansive, attacking style that can lead to high-scoring games but also leaves them vulnerable.

Crucial Tie-Breakers That Matter

This is where it gets interesting, especially when Real Sociedad and Atlético Madrid are neck-and-neck:

  1. Head-to-Head Points: If two teams are level on points, La Liga first looks at the points accumulated in matches played directly between those two teams. The team with more points from their encounters ranks higher. This can be a huge factor for these rivals.

  2. Head-to-Head Goal Difference: If head-to-head points are also equal, the next tie-breaker is the goal difference from their direct matches.

  3. Overall Goal Difference: Only if more than two teams are tied, OR if the head-to-head criteria can't separate two teams, does overall goal difference (total goals scored minus total goals conceded across all league games) come into play.

  4. Goals Scored: If still tied, the team with more total goals scored in the league ranks higher.

This strict hierarchy means that a single match between Real Sociedad and Atlético Madrid can have double the impact, influencing both their direct points tally and potential tie-breaker scenarios later in the season. It’s a tactical chess match that coaches plan for months in advance.
