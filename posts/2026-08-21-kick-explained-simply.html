Getting Started on Kick: Your First Steps

Ready to explore Kick for yourself? Whether you're new to streaming or transitioning from another platform, getting set up and live is straightforward. Here’s a simple breakdown to get you broadcasting.

  1. Create Your Kick Account:

    Visit Kick.com and sign up. It’s a quick process for an email, username, and password. Choose a memorable username, and verify your email.

  2. Configure Your Channel Settings:

    In your Creator Dashboard, set up your channel page. Upload a profile picture, banner, write an "About Me," and link social media. Personalise your page to reflect your content.

  3. Set Up Your Stream Key:

    Find your unique Stream Key in your dashboard. This connects your streaming software to Kick. Never share this key. Copy it carefully.

  4. Choose and Configure Your Streaming Software:

    You'll need broadcasting software like OBS Studio or Streamlabs.

    Open your software, go to 'Stream' settings, select 'Custom' or 'Kick.com,' and paste your Stream Key. Optimise video/audio for your internet and hardware for a smooth viewer experience.

  5. Go Live and Engage:

    Before going live in your software, set your stream title, category, and tags in your Kick dashboard. Once live, engage with your chat! A vibrant, interactive community is the lifeblood of any successful stream.
